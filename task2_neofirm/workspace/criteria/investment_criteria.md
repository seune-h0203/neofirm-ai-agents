# NeoFirm Investment Criteria
**Sector:** AI + Expert Hybrid College Admissions Consulting
**Date:** 2026-10-09

## Scoring Rubric

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Weak | Evidence absent or strongly negative |
| 2 | Below threshold | Evidence limited or significant concerns |
| 3 | Moderate | Mixed evidence; plausible but unproven |
| 4 | Strong | Well-supported with clear evidence |
| 5 | Exceptional | Evidence is definitive and differentiated |

---

## 10 Investment Criteria

### 1. problem_severity
**Question:** How severe, frequent, and structural is the customer pain?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Pain is mild or easily resolved without professional help |
| 2 | Pain exists but customers manage with free resources |
| 3 | Real pain but market tolerance for it is high |
| 4 | Structural pain with clear high-stakes consequences |
| 5 | Irreversible, high-cost, high-frequency pain with no adequate solution |

**Notes for reviewers:** Consider E01 (불가역성), E03 (구조적 문제), E06 (시즌 반복성). Score reflects both severity AND structural nature.

---

### 2. customer_evidence
**Question:** What validated evidence exists that customers will pay for this solution?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | No customer data, no interviews, no LOIs |
| 2 | Anecdotal only; no signed commitments |
| 3 | Qualitative interviews; some interest signals |
| 4 | LOIs or pilot agreements from target segment |
| 5 | Paid pilots, active customers, measurable retention |

**Notes for reviewers:** E13 (전환율 미검증) applies. Evaluate whether source contains any actual customer validation data.

---

### 3. market_opportunity
**Question:** Is the total addressable market large enough to justify investment?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Niche market with limited growth potential |
| 2 | Small market or highly fragmented |
| 3 | Moderate size; competitive dynamics unclear |
| 4 | Large market with clear underserved segment |
| 5 | Large, growing market with structural shift opportunity |

**Notes for reviewers:** Korean college admissions consulting market. Consider seasonality, annual cohort size (고3 학생 수), and premium segment pricing opportunity.

---

### 4. differentiation
**Question:** Is NeoFirm's model defensible vs incumbent consultants and AI-only tools?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | No differentiation; easily replicated |
| 2 | Minor differentiation; easy to copy |
| 3 | Some differentiation but not durable |
| 4 | Clear hybrid model advantage; data moat potential |
| 5 | Strong, multi-layer moat (data + expertise + network) already building |

**Notes for reviewers:** E08, E09 (data flywheel), E11 (AI+전문가 신뢰 구조). Compare to pure AI solutions and traditional 1:1 consulting.

---

### 5. technical_feasibility
**Question:** Is the AI automation technically achievable with current technology?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Core AI tasks not feasible with current technology |
| 2 | Technically possible but major engineering challenges |
| 3 | Feasible with standard LLM/ML stack; significant tuning needed |
| 4 | Core tasks (data analysis, candidate generation) clearly doable |
| 5 | Proven tech stack; similar systems already exist at scale |

**Notes for reviewers:** E04 (AI Scale 업무 목록). Evaluate each specific AI task: data collection, grade analysis, university comparison, probability scoring, portfolio generation, monitoring, simulation.

---

### 6. execution_feasibility
**Question:** Does the team have the capability to execute this model?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | No evidence of any founding team or relevant expertise |
| 2 | Team exists but lacks critical domain expertise |
| 3 | Some relevant expertise; key gaps remain |
| 4 | Team with both technical and domain expertise confirmed |
| 5 | Experienced founding team with verified track record |

**Notes for reviewers:** E14 (전문가 확보 미검증). The source describes the model design; evaluate evidence of actual team capability.

---

### 7. business_model
**Question:** Is the revenue model sustainable and economics favorable?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | No clear revenue model |
| 2 | Revenue model unclear or unfavorable unit economics |
| 3 | Reasonable model but unit economics unproven |
| 4 | Clear model (free→paid funnel) with plausible LTV |
| 5 | Proven conversion funnel with strong unit economics validated |

**Notes for reviewers:** E07 (GTM 5단계), E10 (정보 아닌 결과 판매). Evaluate the freemium → consulting conversion model quality and LTV/CAC dynamics.

---

### 8. scalability
**Question:** Can this business scale without linear cost growth?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Each additional customer requires proportional expert time |
| 2 | Limited scalability; bottleneck in expert capacity |
| 3 | Moderate AI leverage; some expert scaling required |
| 4 | AI handles most volume; expert adds final judgment only |
| 5 | AI scales automatically; expert review per session, not per data point |

**Notes for reviewers:** E04, E05 (AI vs 전문가 역할 분리). Consider how expert judgment bottleneck is managed as student volume grows.

---

### 9. risk
**Question:** What is the overall risk-adjusted investment thesis?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Fatal risks present (regulatory, trust, ethical) |
| 2 | High and multiple significant risks |
| 3 | Moderate risks; manageable with known mitigations |
| 4 | Risks identified with clear mitigation strategies |
| 5 | Low risk profile; risks are known and controlled |

**Notes for reviewers:** E11 (신뢰 리스크), E12 (개인정보), E14 (전문가 확보). Weight both market/execution risks and structural risks.

---

### 10. evidence_quality
**Question:** How strong and primary is the evidence base for this investment thesis?

| Score | Admissions Consulting Definition |
|-------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | Claims only; no supporting evidence |
| 2 | Internal claims; no external validation |
| 3 | Logical reasoning supported by general market knowledge |
| 4 | Source-backed evidence with some external validation |
| 5 | Comprehensive evidence: customer data, market data, pilot results |

**Notes for reviewers:** Label each cited evidence item as SOURCE-SUPPORTED FACT, ASSUMPTION, or PROPOSAL. E13, E14, E15 are explicitly unvalidated assumptions.

---

## Instructions for Reviewers

1. Each reviewer evaluates **only their assigned 5 criteria**
2. Cite evidence by ID (E01–E16)
3. Label assumptions explicitly: write **[ASSUMPTION]** next to unvalidated claims
4. Label source-supported facts: write **[SOURCE]** next to claims backed by NLM responses
5. Provide a provisional decision (INVEST / HOLD / PASS) with MEDIUM/HIGH confidence
6. Do NOT share scores with other reviewers before completing your own review
